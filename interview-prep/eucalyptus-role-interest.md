# What interests you most about this role / working for Eucalyptus?

## Suggested Answer

Three things genuinely excite me about Eucalyptus:

### 1. Mission-Driven Healthcare Impact

What drew me in first is that Eucalyptus is solving real healthcare accessibility problems — connecting patients with practitioners and pharmacists through a seamless telehealth platform. Having worked on products at scale (youXinsights serving 10+ organisations, loan matching at youXpowered), I know the difference between building software that just works and software that meaningfully changes outcomes for people. Eucalyptus does the latter — over 500,000 patients helped across brands like Pilot, Juniper, Kin, and Software is a tangible impact I want to be part of.

### 2. Technical Challenges That Match My Strengths

The engineering problems at Eucalyptus are exactly the kind I thrive on:

- **Multi-brand, configurable platform architecture** — At youXpowered, I architected youXinsights to replace Power BI across 10+ organisations, each with different reporting needs. Eucalyptus runs multiple brands (Pilot, Juniper, Kin, Software, Compound) on a shared platform, which is a similar multi-tenant challenge I find deeply engaging.
- **Microservices and real-time systems** — I built real-time data visualization with 60-second refresh intervals and cross-filtering across 25+ report types. Eucalyptus's microservices architecture with federated GraphQL, Node.js/Go services, and Pub/Sub is a natural evolution of what I've been doing.
- **High deployment velocity** — Eucalyptus deploys to production hundreds of times per month with code hitting production within minutes of merge. I love working in fast-shipping environments where you see the impact of your work immediately.
- **Tech stack alignment** — TypeScript, React, Node.js, and cloud infrastructure (I've worked with AWS; Eucalyptus uses GCP) are my core tools. The stack feels like home.

### 3. Growth Trajectory and Scale

Eucalyptus is at an exciting inflection point — A$120M+ in revenue, expanding across 9 countries, and the recent Hims & Hers acquisition validates the business at a $1B+ level. Joining at this stage means working on genuinely global-scale problems (international expansion, multi-region infrastructure, regulatory compliance across markets) while still being close enough to the core that individual engineers have outsized impact.

## What NOT to Say

- Don't lead with the acquisition / financial upside — it sounds mercenary
- Don't be generic ("I like the culture") without specifics
- Don't undersell by saying "I want to learn" — frame it as "I want to contribute AND grow"

## Sample Closing Line

> "Ultimately, I want to be at a company where the engineering challenges are hard, the mission matters, and individual engineers can make a real difference. Eucalyptus checks all three."
